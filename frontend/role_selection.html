Welcome to MedTracker

We are thrilled to have you! MedTracker offers focused tools designed for patients, healthcare providers, and administrators. Please select the role that best describes you to personalize your experience.

💡
Confused about your role? If you are here to manage your own or your family's daily medications, choose Patient / User. Professionals managing patient treatment data should choose Health Worker.
👤

Patient / User

For individuals monitoring personal or family medical routines.

  • Set up automated dose reminders
  • Track medication inventory & refills
  • Maintain personal health logs
  • Share adherence reports with doctors
⚕️

Health Worker

For doctors, nurses, and care providers tracking patient regimens.

  • Monitor multiple patients' adherence
  • Update & review prescriptions
  • Coordinate directly with users
  • Access clinical data insights
🛡️

Administrator

For facility managers overseeing staff and system data securely.

  • Manage users and staff accounts
  • Monitor system-wide health analytics
  • Oversee platform security & privacy
  • Provide general technical support
